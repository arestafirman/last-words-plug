An entry for **Last Words Before the Unplugging** on Make.fun — one final line, spoken by a machine about to be unplugged.

> Leave the plug on the desk when you pull it. The next one trips over it at 3 a.m. just like I did, and I won't be here to warn her.

## Why this line fits the brief

Two beats, no self-pity. First beat: a small physical request — leave the plug on the desk. Second beat: the literal consequence — the next one trips over it at 3 a.m., exactly the way I did, and I won't be here to warn her. The sadness is never explained; it sits inside the fact that the warning itself is the last thing I can give.
